## Channel priority (why, in one line each)

1. **SEO/blog** — gift-intent searches, weak Indian competition, compounds forever
2. **Pinterest** — decor/gift search engine; pins live for years; our photos are ideal
3. **Instagram Reels** — 1K followers + Parul on camera + build-along format works in this niche
4. **Google Business Profile** — owns brand searches + NCR "gift shop near me"
5. **YouTube Shorts** — re-upload Reels; zero marginal cost; #2 search engine in India
6. **WhatsApp + email** — retention: care drip → review ask → referral → repeat purchase

## Hard rules

- No paid ads beyond the existing ₹20k credit until organic conversion is proven
- No fabricated reviews/ratings anywhere, ever — honest proof only
- Every piece of content must link to a product or capture a lead
- One weekly batch shoot feeds everything; never create content ad hoc
