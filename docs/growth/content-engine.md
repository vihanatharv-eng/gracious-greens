# The Content Engine — one shoot, many assets

**Input:** one 90-min batch shoot per week (see sprint-1-founder-tasks.md for the shot list).
**Output:** a full week of content across every channel. All repurposing (cuts, captions, pins, posts) is produced by Claude from the raw clips — founder time stays at 5–7 hrs/week.

## The pipeline

```
90-min shoot
 ├─ 3–4 Reels (15–30s cuts)          → Instagram, then re-upload to YouTube Shorts
 ├─ 6–10 photos                       → 2–3 Pinterest pins, 1 GBP post, WhatsApp status
 ├─ 1 story sequence                  → IG Stories (build progress, poll, link)
 └─ imagery for the week's blog post  → /journal article
```

## Reel templates (rotate weekly, don't invent new formats)

1. **Build-along timelapse** — bowl → moss → figurines → reveal. Text hook: "Someone ordered this for their parents' 40th anniversary…" End: "Custom scenes at graciousgreens.in"
2. **Care tip** (15s) — one plant, one tip, Parul's voice. Hook: "Your ZZ plant's yellow leaves aren't thirst —"
3. **Packing an order** — bubble wrap, handwritten card, box seal. Highest-trust format; converts lurkers.
4. **The story behind a piece** — Parul to camera, 30s, why this customer ordered it (with permission).

## Hooks that work in this niche (first 2 seconds decide everything)

- "This gift made a grown man cry." (story)
- "POV: your friend remembers everything you love." (custom scene)
- "Stop killing your plants. Do this instead." (care)
- "₹1,299 vs a bouquet that dies in 4 days." (comparison)
- "Packing order #47 — going to Bangalore 🚚" (social proof)

## Caption formula

Hook line → 2-line story → 1 care/craft detail → CTA ("Custom scenes: link in bio / graciousgreens.in") → 5–8 niche hashtags (#miniaturegarden #plantgifts #deskplants #handmadeindia #gifting — rotate, never 30 generic ones).

## WhatsApp retention drip (send manually post-delivery; templates)

- **Day 1:** "Your scene has reached! 🌿 Here's your care guide: graciousgreens.in/downloads/gracious-greens-plant-care-guide.pdf — message me anytime if the plant looks unhappy."
- **Day 7:** "One week in! Quick check — how's the [plant] doing? Send a photo if you'd like me to take a look."
- **Day 30:** "A month with your scene 🎉 If you've loved it, a Google review would mean the world: [link]. And if anyone you know has a birthday coming up — we make gifts with their story in them."
