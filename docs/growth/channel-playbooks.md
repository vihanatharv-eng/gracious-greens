# Channel Playbooks

## Pinterest (Sprint 2 setup — biggest untapped channel)

**Why:** Pinterest is a search engine for gifts/decor; pins surface for years; India usage growing; our white-studio + lifestyle photo pairs are exactly the format that performs.

**Setup (once):**
1. Business account at pinterest.com/business, claim graciousgreens.in (adds attribution + enables Rich Pins — our Product schema already qualifies)
2. Create 8 boards: Miniature Garden Ideas · Plant Gift Ideas · Desk Plants & Decor · Housewarming Gifts India · Corporate Gifting Ideas · Plant Care Tips · Spiritual Home Decor · Behind the Scenes (Handmade)
3. Board descriptions = keyword-rich 2 sentences each (Claude drafts)

**Weekly (30 min, batchable):** 5 pins — 3 product (white-bg + lifestyle), 1 care-tip graphic, 1 build photo. Every pin: keyword title (e.g. "Miniature Family Garden Planter — Personalised Plant Gift India"), 2-sentence description with keywords, link to the exact product page.

## Instagram

- Bio: keep wa.me link + site link; highlights already set (covers in Downloads/GG_Highlights)
- 3 Reels/week from templates (content-engine.md) — consistency beats virality
- Reply to every comment within a day; DMs are sales conversations, answer with product links
- Monthly: one collab/giveaway with a micro plant/decor account (barter, not paid)
- UGC: ask every customer for a photo (Day 7 WhatsApp message); reshare to Stories + save to a "Your Homes" highlight

## Google Business Profile

- Weekly post (photo + 2 lines + product link) — drafts come from the batch shoot
- Reviews: reply to all within 24h; target +5/month via the Day 30 WhatsApp ask
- Photos: add 3–5/week from shoots (profiles with fresh photos get more discovery)
- Keep category "Gift shop" primary; add "Plant nursery" secondary

## YouTube Shorts

- Channel: "Gracious Greens — Miniature Plant Scenes"
- Re-upload the week's 3 Reels; titles = search phrases ("How I Build a Miniature Garden Scene", "ZZ Plant Care in 30 Seconds")
- Description: 2 lines + graciousgreens.in link. Zero extra production cost.

## WhatsApp

- **Broadcast list** (not group): add every customer + subscriber who opts in; 1 message/week max — new piece, care tip, or seasonal reminder
- Retention drip: Day 1 / 7 / 30 templates in content-engine.md
- Referral (Sprint 3): "Gift a friend ₹100 off their first scene; you get ₹100 off your next" — tracked manually via names, no tooling needed at this scale

## The ₹20k Google Ads credit (temporary, learning tool)

- Keep Search-only campaigns running until credit exhausts; harvest the **search-terms report** monthly — every converting query becomes a blog/collection keyword target
- When credit ends: pause everything. Do not add money until organic conversion is proven and AOV justifies CAC.
